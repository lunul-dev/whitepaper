Frequently asked questions

Clear answers.
No unnecessary jargon.

What is Lunul?
Lunul is building a financial privacy ecosystem centred on NullNet, its privacy infrastructure, and Terminull, its financial management suite. The goal is to make financial discretion more practical for individuals and businesses using blockchain.
What is NullNet?
NullNet is the privacy infrastructure in the Lunul ecosystem. It is designed to combine blockchain execution with a dedicated zero-knowledge privacy environment for supported financial services.
What is Terminull?
Terminull is a financial management suite designed around the Lunul ecosystem. Intended applications include account management, business payment workflows, payroll administration, escrow, treasury organisation and approval controls. Availability depends on implementation and release status.
Why does financial privacy matter?
Public blockchain activity can reveal balances, transaction histories and financial relationships. Privacy gives legitimate users greater discretion over what becomes publicly visible.
Does privacy mean hiding from authorities?
No. Lunul does not position its services as a way to evade law enforcement, sanctions or financial regulation. Any screening, cooperation or disclosure must reflect applicable law and the actual capabilities of the service.
Can Lunul guarantee complete anonymity?
No. Privacy depends on the protocol, transaction path, wallet security, external services and other potential sources of information. NullNet aims to reduce unnecessary exposure for supported activity, not to guarantee that every transaction is untraceable in every circumstance.
Do users keep control of their wallet keys?
The intended model supports users retaining control of their own wallet keys. Users should confirm custody arrangements and signing requirements for the specific product or deployment they use.
Can businesses use Terminull for payroll and treasury?
Terminull is designed with business workflows in mind, including payroll, account organisation, escrow and approval controls. Production use depends on which features have been implemented, tested and enabled.
Can third-party developers build directly on the privacy layer?
The dedicated zero-knowledge environment is intended to serve Terminull's services rather than operate as an unrestricted public developer platform. Consult the technical documentation for confirmed developer capabilities.
Is Terminull a traditional bank?
Terminull is presented as a blockchain-based financial management suite. It should not be confused with a licensed deposit-taking bank unless the relevant legal entity holds the necessary authorisation. Check service terms, custody arrangements and regulatory status before using any financial service.
